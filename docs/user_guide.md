# User Guide

1. Register or sign in at `/`.
2. Click **Start New Research** on the dashboard.
3. Enter the company name and (optionally) an industry and known competitors.
4. Choose a research depth: **Quick**, **Standard**, or **Deep**.
5. Submit. You will be redirected to the progress page.
6. When the pipeline completes, click **View Report** to see the full analysis.
7. Export the report as Markdown or JSON.
8. Add companies to your watchlist for periodic re-checks.
9. Browse the Intelligence Feed to spot trends across all research runs.
